# Photo Booth

- **Title:** Virtual photo booth: attendees photograph themselves and post to a gallery
- **Date:** 2026-11-14
- **Participants:** Unknown (a brainstorm in chat with one requester)
- **Source:** Inline: a one-sentence request pasted into a Winnow run, with the questions answered in chat.
- **Tickets raised:**
  - [#5 Photo Booth: take a photo with your laptop camera and post it to a shared gallery](https://github.com/nimble-software/nam-2026-conference/issues/5)
- **Context updated:** `product-context/prd.md`: Photo Booth is an offering, photos are public, the poster can delete their own, there is no moderation, and the gallery starts empty.
- **Set aside:**
  - Frames and filters, likes and comments: possible later tickets, not in the first version.
  - A file-picker fallback when the camera is unavailable: a blocked or missing camera shows a message instead.
  - Moderation before publishing: needs a reviewer role the app does not have.
  - Seeded placeholder photos: they would show people who do not exist.
- **Q&A:**
  - One page or two? One page, `/photo-booth`, with the gallery below the camera.
  - Can a poster take a photo down, and is anything checked before it goes live? The poster can delete their own; no moderation.
  - Does the seed include photos, and which clock stamps them? Empty gallery; times come from the conference clock.
  - Fallback or extras? Neither.
  - Approved with "go".
